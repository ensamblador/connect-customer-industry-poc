International roaming: general information
==========================================

Roaming lets you use your phone when you travel outside the country. Availability and cost depend on your plan and the country you visit.

Can I use my phone when I travel abroad?
----------------------------------------

Generally yes, if your plan includes international roaming. Some plans already include it for certain countries, and in other cases you can add a travel pass before you leave. It is worth checking before the trip.

How do I avoid unexpected charges abroad?
-----------------------------------------

Before traveling, confirm whether your plan includes the destination country or whether you need a pass. You can also turn off data roaming in your phone settings and use Wi-Fi to connect. That way you control your usage while you are away.

Do calls and messages work the same while roaming?
--------------------------------------------------

They can work, but the rates are usually different from those in your home country. Sending messages or using messaging apps over Wi-Fi is usually the most economical option while traveling.

Do I need to activate anything before traveling?
------------------------------------------------

In many cases roaming activates on its own when you arrive at your destination, if your plan includes it. If you added a travel pass, it activates according to the dates you chose. To confirm your case, a representative can review the options.

Roaming conditions vary by country and by plan. Check the details before traveling or ask a representative.
