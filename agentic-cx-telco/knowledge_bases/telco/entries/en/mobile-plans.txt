Mobile plans and what they include
==================================

We offer mobile plans for every use, from basic plans with enough data for everyday needs to unlimited plans with more benefits. Every plan includes unlimited calls and messages within the country.

Which plans are available?
--------------------------

We have three main tiers. The Basic plan, starting at twenty dollars a month, includes a fixed amount of data. The Plus plan offers more data and speed for people who use video and social media. The Unlimited plan gives data with no monthly cap and access to the 5G network where it is available.

Are calls and messages unlimited?
---------------------------------

Yes. Every plan includes unlimited calls and text messages within the country. International calls may have a different cost depending on the destination.

What happens if I use all my data?
----------------------------------

On plans with fixed data, once you reach the limit your speed is reduced for the rest of the month, but you stay connected. The Unlimited plan has no monthly data cutoff.

Can I add lines for my family?
------------------------------

Yes. You can add several lines to the same account, and the price per line usually drops the more lines you have. For changes to your account, a representative can help.

Prices and data amounts are general references and may change. For the current offer, check the website or ask a representative.
