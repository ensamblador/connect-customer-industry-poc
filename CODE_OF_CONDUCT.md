# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a place to build and learn together. Everyone who takes part in
it — filing issues, reviewing code, discussing designs — should be able to do so
without harassment or hostility, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Assume good faith, and give others room to learn.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and say so plainly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about any person or group.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks these rules, report it
privately to the maintainers rather than in a public issue. Reports are handled
confidentially, and we will not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in
good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for applying this code of conduct. They may edit or
remove contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently block
anyone whose behavior they judge to be harmful to the project or its community.
