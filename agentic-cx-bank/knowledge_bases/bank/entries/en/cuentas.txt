Bank accounts and what they include
===================================

We offer accounts for every need, from savings accounts made for everyday use to checking accounts with more benefits. All accounts include online banking and a mobile app at no extra cost.

What types of account are available?
------------------------------------

We have three main types. The Savings account is made for setting money aside and usually pays interest on the balance. The Checking account is ideal for daily use, with a debit card and frequent payments. The Payroll account receives your salary directly and usually comes with added benefits.

Do I need a minimum balance to open an account?
------------------------------------------------

It depends on the type of account. Some savings accounts have no minimum balance, while other premium accounts do ask you to keep an average balance. A representative can tell you the current requirements for the account you are interested in.

How do I check my balance and transactions?
-------------------------------------------

You can review your balance and transactions from online banking or the mobile app at any time. You can also do it at an ATM or a branch. For security, viewing specific details of your account requires you to sign in or verify your identity.

Can I have more than one account?
---------------------------------

Yes. You can have several accounts linked to the same customer, for example a savings and a checking account. To open a new account or make changes to your account, a representative can help.

Requirements, rates, and benefits are general references and may change. To learn the current offer, check the website or ask a representative.
