Activate card: what it is and how to do it
==========================================

When you receive a new card, whether debit or credit, you need to activate it before you can use it. Activation confirms that the card reached you and makes it ready for purchases and withdrawals.

Why do I have to activate my card?
----------------------------------

Activation is a security measure. While the card is not activated, it cannot be used, even if someone else had it. By activating it, you confirm that you are the one who received it and you are ready to use it.

How do I activate my card?
--------------------------

You can activate it from the mobile app or online banking, in the cards section, by choosing the activate option. You can also do it at an ATM the first time you use it with your PIN, or with help from a representative. Activation usually takes only a few minutes.

How do I set or change my card PIN?
-----------------------------------

During activation you can choose a PIN, or change it later from the app or at an ATM. Choose a number that is not easy to guess and do not share it with anyone. Never write your PIN on the card.

What do I do if my card does not activate?
------------------------------------------

Check that the card details were entered correctly and that it is the most recent card you received. If it still does not activate, a representative can help you review the status and complete the activation.

The exact steps may vary by card type and channel. If you have questions, a representative can guide you.
