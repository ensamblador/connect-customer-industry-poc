Transfers and payments
======================

You can send and receive money in several ways, from transfers between accounts at the same bank to transfers to other banks. Most are done from online banking or the mobile app.

What types of transfer are there?
---------------------------------

There are internal transfers, between accounts at the same bank, which are usually instant. There are also transfers to other banks, which may use the country's interbank network and arrive in minutes or on the same business day, depending on the time and the destination.

How long does a transfer take to arrive?
-----------------------------------------

Transfers between accounts at the same bank usually show right away. Transfers to other banks depend on the network and the time; those sent outside business hours or on weekends may process on the next business day.

Are there limits on transfers?
------------------------------

Yes, for security accounts have daily and per-transaction limits. These limits vary by account type and channel. You can check your limits in the app; to adjust them, a representative can guide you on the available options.

What details do I need to transfer to another person?
-----------------------------------------------------

Generally you need the account number or destination identifier and the receiving bank. Always double-check the details before you confirm, since a sent transfer can be hard to recover. If you have questions, a representative can help.

Times, limits, and requirements are general references and may change. To learn the current terms, check the website or ask a representative.
