Debit and credit cards
======================

We offer debit cards linked to your account and credit cards with different limits and benefits. All cards include contactless technology and can be managed from the mobile app.

What is the difference between debit and credit?
------------------------------------------------

A debit card uses the money available in your account and deducts each purchase right away. A credit card lets you buy with a line of credit and pay later, either in full or in installments, depending on your card's terms.

How do I keep my card secure?
-----------------------------

Never share your PIN or your full card details. From the app you can temporarily lock or unlock the card, turn on purchase alerts, and set limits. If you do not recognize a transaction, contact a representative as soon as possible.

What do I do if I lose my card or it is stolen?
-----------------------------------------------

Lock it immediately from the app or online banking to prevent unauthorized use. Then a representative can help you request a replacement card. Locking the card does not close your account or affect your other products.

Can I use my card abroad?
-------------------------

Yes, most cards work abroad for purchases and withdrawals. Currency conversion charges may apply depending on the type of card. Before traveling, it is a good idea to review your limits and turn on travel notifications.

Benefits, limits, and charges are general references and may change. To learn your card's terms, check the website or ask a representative.
