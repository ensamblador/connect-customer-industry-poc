Frequently asked questions
==========================

Answers to the most common questions about using your account, online banking, and support.

I just opened my account and cannot sign in to online banking. What do I do?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first step is to check that you are using the correct username and that your password is typed correctly. If this is your first sign-in, you may need to activate access from the link or message you received. If it still does not work, a representative can help enable your access.

How do I recover my password?
-----------------------------

From the sign-in screen you can use the option to recover or reset your password. We will ask you to verify your identity before creating a new one. For security, never share your password or your verification codes with anyone.

How do I turn on notifications for my transactions?
---------------------------------------------------

From the mobile app you can turn on alerts for purchases, withdrawals, and transfers. Notifications help you catch any transaction you do not recognize in time.

Does online banking work all day?
---------------------------------

Yes, you can check your information and bank online twenty-four hours a day. Some transactions to other banks may process on the next business day depending on the time you make them.

How do I change the language of support?
----------------------------------------

You can request support in English at any time. If you prefer another language, a representative can help or transfer you to someone who speaks it.

If your question is about your specific account balance, a particular transaction, or a problem with your card, a representative with access to those systems can help you best.
