Fees and charges
================

We aim to keep fees clear and easy to understand. Many everyday transactions have no cost, and others may carry a charge depending on the type of account or service. Below are the most common fees.

What fees can my account have?
------------------------------

The most common fees are account maintenance, card issuance or replacement, and certain withdrawals at out-of-network ATMs. Many accounts waive maintenance if you meet conditions such as receiving your payroll or keeping an average balance.

Do you charge for using ATMs?
-----------------------------

Withdrawals at ATMs in our own network are usually free. At out-of-network ATMs a per-transaction charge may apply. The app shows the ATMs in the network before you withdraw so you can avoid charges.

Do transfers have a fee?
------------------------

Transfers between accounts at the same bank are usually free. Transfers to other banks may carry a charge depending on the account type and the channel used. You can review the detail before confirming the transaction.

How can I avoid fees?
---------------------

In general, it helps to use the digital channels, withdraw at in-network ATMs, and meet the conditions that waive maintenance. A representative can explain which conditions apply to your account to reduce or avoid charges.

Fees are general references and may change. To learn the current fee schedule, check the website or ask a representative.
