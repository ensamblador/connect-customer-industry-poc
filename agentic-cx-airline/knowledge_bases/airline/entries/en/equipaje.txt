Baggage: carry-on, checked, and special items
=============================================

AeroLatam sets your baggage allowance based on your fare and your AeroLatam Club tier. Check your limits before you travel to avoid fees at the airport.

What can I bring as carry-on?
-----------------------------

All fares include one personal item and one carry-on piece of up to 10 kg, with maximum dimensions of 55 x 35 x 25 cm. It must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you.

How much checked baggage do I get?
----------------------------------

The Light fare does not include checked baggage; Plus includes one piece of up to 23 kg and Top includes two pieces of 23 kg. The limit per piece is 158 cm adding height, width, and length.

What about excess or special baggage?
-------------------------------------

Extra baggage or pieces over the allowed weight carry a per-piece fee that is cheaper paid online than at the airport. Instruments, sports equipment, and other special items require advance notice and may have specific fees.

Which items are prohibited?
---------------------------

Explosives, gases, flammable liquids, and objects that could be used as a weapon are not allowed in the cabin. Lithium batteries and aerosols are restricted; check the full list before packing.

How do I protect my valuables?
------------------------------

Carry money, documents, medication, and electronics in your carry-on. AeroLatam is not responsible for valuables placed in checked baggage.
