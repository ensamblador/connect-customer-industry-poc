AeroLatam general FAQ
=====================

Here you will find answers to the most common questions about traveling with AeroLatam. For specific cases, a representative can help through our support channels.

How do I contact AeroLatam?
---------------------------

You can reach us through the chat on the website and app, call our customer center, or visit a counter at the airport. Chat and phone are available every day.

What happens if my flight changes?
----------------------------------

If we change your time or route, we notify you by email and message. You can accept the new option, choose another available flight at no cost, or request a refund when the change is significant.

What documents do I need to travel?
-----------------------------------

For domestic flights a valid ID is enough. For international flights you need a valid passport and, depending on the destination, a visa and additional documents. Check the requirements of the country you are traveling to.

Can minors travel alone?
------------------------

Unaccompanied minors travel under our escort service, with documentation and authorization from parents or guardians. Request the service when booking, as it has specific conditions and a fee.

Can I travel with pets?
-----------------------

We accept pets in the cabin or in the hold depending on their size and the route, with advance booking and limited space per flight. The pet must travel in a suitable carrier and with valid health documents.

Do you offer special assistance?
--------------------------------

Yes. We offer wheelchairs, priority boarding, and support for passengers with reduced mobility or other needs. Request it at least 48 hours in advance.
