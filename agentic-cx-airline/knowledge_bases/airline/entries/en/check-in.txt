Check-in, boarding, and seats
=============================

Check-in confirms you are traveling and issues your boarding pass. With AeroLatam you can check in online, in the app, or at the airport.

When can I check in?
--------------------

Online and app check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before on domestic flights and 90 minutes before on international flights. At the airport, counters open 3 hours before and close 45 minutes before departure.

What time is boarding?
----------------------

Boarding usually starts 40 minutes before departure and the gate closes 15 minutes before takeoff. Arrive at the gate early, since boarding is not possible after the gate closes.

How do I choose my seat?
------------------------

You can select a seat when booking or during check-in. Top fares include free selection; on Light and Plus there may be a fee depending on the location. If you do not choose, the system assigns a seat at check-in.

What is priority boarding?
--------------------------

AeroLatam Club Oro and Platino members, Top fare passengers, and travelers needing special assistance board first. This speeds up carry-on stowage and boarding of the aircraft.

Do I need a document to board?
------------------------------

Yes. Present your boarding pass and a valid ID; on international flights, a passport and visa where required. Check that your documents are valid before you travel.
