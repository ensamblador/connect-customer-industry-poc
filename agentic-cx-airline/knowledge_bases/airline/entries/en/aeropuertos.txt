Airports, counters, and hours
=============================

AeroLatam operates from the main cities across Latin America. Here we explain where to find us at the airport and our service hours.

Where are the AeroLatam counters?
---------------------------------

Our counters are located in the departures area of each airport, marked with the AeroLatam logo. In larger terminals we have separate areas for baggage drop and for Top fare and Club member service.

What time do the counters open?
-------------------------------

Counters open 3 hours before each departure and close 45 minutes before on domestic flights and 60 minutes before on international flights. We recommend arriving early for security and immigration.

Are there self-service kiosks?
------------------------------

Yes. The self-service kiosks let you check in, print your boarding pass, and print your baggage tags. You then leave your bags at the fast baggage drop counter, without long lines.

Does AeroLatam have lounges?
----------------------------

We have lounges at selected airports, with access for Platino members, Top fare passengers, and holders of certain co-branded cards. They offer food, drinks, wi-fi, and rest areas.

What if my flight leaves from another terminal?
-----------------------------------------------

Check the terminal on your itinerary or on the airport screens, as it may change. If in doubt, AeroLatam staff at the counter will guide you to the correct gate.
