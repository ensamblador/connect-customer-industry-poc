AeroLatam Club: frequent flyer program
=======================================

AeroLatam Club is our loyalty program. You earn miles when you fly and with our partners, and you redeem them for tickets, class upgrades, and other benefits. Enrollment is free on the website or in the app.

How do I earn miles?
--------------------

You earn miles based on the distance flown and the fare class purchased. You also earn miles with hotels, car rentals, and purchases on the co-branded cards. Add your membership number when booking so miles are credited automatically.

How do I redeem my miles?
-------------------------

In "Use miles" you choose an award flight or a class upgrade based on availability. You can pay with miles only or combine miles and cash. Redemptions are confirmed instantly and appear on your itinerary.

Membership tiers
----------------

The program has three tiers: Clásico, the entry level; Oro, once you pass an annual miles threshold, with extra baggage and priority boarding; and Platino, the top level, with lounge access, flexible changes, and preferred service.

Co-branded travel cards
-----------------------

The AeroLatam Club cards, issued with partner banks, earn miles on every purchase and offer welcome miles. Some include free baggage and lounge access depending on the card level.

When do miles expire?
---------------------

Miles expire 24 months after they are credited if there is no activity on the account. Any earning or redemption resets the clock. Platino members may have extended validity conditions.
