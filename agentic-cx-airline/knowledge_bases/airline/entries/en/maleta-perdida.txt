Lost or delayed baggage: how to report it
==========================================

If your baggage does not arrive on the belt, AeroLatam helps you locate it and deliver it to you. Follow these steps to report your lost or delayed bag.

Step 1: confirm the bag did not arrive
--------------------------------------

Wait until all the bags from your flight come out. If yours does not appear, gather your flight information (flight number, date, and origin) and the baggage claim tag you received when you checked it in.

Step 2: go to the Baggage Service counter
-----------------------------------------

Head to the AeroLatam Baggage Service counter in the arrivals area, before you leave the baggage claim zone. You can also start the report online on our website or in the app.

Step 3: present the claim tag and describe the bag
--------------------------------------------------

Hand over the baggage claim tag and describe your bag: size, color, brand, and any distinguishing features. These details help identify it quickly in the system.

Step 4: register the report and get your PIR
--------------------------------------------

The agent registers a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) and gives you a PIR number. Keep it, as it is your reference for following up on the case.

Step 5: provide a delivery address and contact
----------------------------------------------

Give an address for delivery and a phone number or email for contact. When we find your bag, we send it to the registered address at no cost to you.

Step 6: track it with your PIR number
-------------------------------------

Check the status of your baggage with the PIR number on our website, in the app, or with a representative. In case of a long delay or damage, you can request compensation according to the current conditions.
